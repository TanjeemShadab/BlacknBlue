# Copywriting: Voice & Tone Guidelines - Black n Blue Events Logistics

## Brand Personality

**Brand Name:** Black n Blue Events Logistics

**Core Personality Keywords:**
* Professional and Reliable
* High-Energy and Energetic
* Client-Focused and Supportive
* Confident and Authoritative
* Modern and Tech-Savvy

**What we are:**
* A premium partner for grand events.
* A turnkey logistics solution provider.
* Experts in the Dhaka event landscape.

**What we are NOT:**
* Just a rental service (we are logistics partners).
* Casual or unreliable.
* Overly technical without showing the visual results.

---

## Grammar & Mechanics

### Capitalization
* **Headings:** Title Case (e.g., "Our Premium Event Services")
* **UI Elements:** Title Case for Buttons (e.g., "Get A Free Quote")
* **Tagline:** Sentence Case with quotes (e.g., "We are here for your best support")

---

## Terminology Guide

| ❌ Do Not Use | ✅ Use Instead | Reason |
|:-------------|:-------------|:-------|
| Vendor | Partner / Logistics Expert | Establishes a deeper relationship |
| Rental | Solution / Setup | High-end positioning |
| Cheap | Cost-Effective / Competitive | Maintains premium feel |
| Tent | Exhibition Tent / Pagoda | Specific industry terminology |

---

## Tone by Context

### Marketing & Landing Pages
**Tone:** Aspirational, Bold, and Trustworthy.
**Characteristics:** Focus on the scale of transformation (e.g., "From an empty space to a grand stage").

### Hero Section Copy
**Headline:** Black n Blue Events Logistics – Complete Event Solutions in Dhaka
**Tagline:** "We are here for your best support"

### Call to Action (CTA)
* "Get a Free Quote"
* "View Our Projects"
* "Contact Our Experts"
